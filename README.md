## Vipul H. Harihar

**Computer, AI and Technology Scientist and Engineer** · New York, United States

Vipul builds AI systems for regulated finance. At [GenTrust](https://gentrustwm.com/), a New York wealth firm with nearly $6 billion under management, he architected **AIRIA**, an investment operating environment for portfolio oversight, trading support, risk and tax workflows, and **AURA**, a secure enterprise AI agent platform connected to the firm's CRM, portfolio data and documents. Both launched in July 2025.

Before GenTrust he was an Application Developer at IBM and a research engineer at NewYork-Presbyterian and Columbia University Medical Center. He holds an MS in Data Science from Columbia University, where he was a Gold Graduate Marshal, and a BTech in Computer Science from DIT University, where he was the University Gold Medalist.

**Featured:** [A Data Science Alum Was Hired to Fix Code. He Built an Operating System Instead.](https://datascience.columbia.edu/news/data-science-alum-was-hired-fix-code-he-built-operating-system-instead) · Columbia Data Science Institute, 2025
